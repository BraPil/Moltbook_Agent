# Restart Protocol

Purpose
Provide a safe and complete restart procedure when context is nearing limits.

Steps
1. Review the full chat history and ensure all logs are updated and indexed.
2. Summarize and index all work in comprehensive detail.
3. Consult Overall_Plan.md to assess progress, gaps, and next steps.
4. Produce a copy and paste prompt to resume after restart, including an index to key files, logs, and references.
